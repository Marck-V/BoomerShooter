# BoomerShooter

A fast, retro-style first-person shooter built in Godot 4.7. It has PSX-style visuals, a synthwave backdrop, and
movement-first combat in levels built with TrenchBroom.

## Features

- **Movement:** sprint, slide, dash, wall jump, grapple points and launch pads.
- **Weapons:** pistol, rifle, shotgun, rocket launcher, blaster, flamethrower and lightning gun. Each weapon has
  its own upgrades, bought at upgrade stations.
- **Off-hand shield:** block, parry enemy punches to stun them, and throw the shield.
- **Enemies:** melee horde enemies that lunge, jump gaps and throw punch combos, ranged enemies that fire energy
  balls, flyers, dodgers that vanish and sidestep, and shielded variants. Wave triggers spawn enemies when you
  enter a room.
- **Boss:** a boss fight in an arena, with shockwaves, floor hazards and a charge attack.
- **Feedback:** hit flashes, flinches, blood and impact particles, lifesteal orbs, and glowing pickups.

## Status

Work in progress. Level 2 and the boss arena are the most recent areas.
